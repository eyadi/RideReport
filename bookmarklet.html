Step 2 — Create the bookmark

🍎 Safari on iPhone / iPad
  1. 1
    Bookmark this page Tap the Share button (the box with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen → tap Add to Favorites → tap Save.
  2. 2
    Open your Bookmarks Tap the book icon in Safari's toolbar → open Favorites. You should see Strava Dashboard in the list.
  3. 3
    Edit the bookmark URL Swipe left on Strava Dashboard → tap Edit. Tap the URL field at the bottom → tap Select All → tap Paste. Tap Done.
  4. 4
    All done! The bookmark is now the dashboard. Go to strava.com/dashboard, scroll down, and tap the bookmark — the dashboard appears instantly.

⚡ Open Strava Dashboard

← drag this to your bookmarks bar

  1. Go to your Strava feed
    Open strava.com/dashboard in your browser and make sure you are logged in.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of your feed
    Scroll all the way down until you see the Your Recent Activities section. The more you scroll, the more activities the dashboard will capture.
  3. Click the bookmarklet
    Click ⚡ Open Strava Dashboard in your bookmarks bar. The dashboard appears instantly on top of the Strava page.
  4. Give kudos
    Tap ♥ on any activity to send a real kudo. On iPhone you'll feel a native tap. The heart turns to ✓ and the count updates right away.
  5. Open an activity
    Tap the route map thumbnail or the → arrow to open any activity. Want it to open in the Strava app instead? Tap ⚙ in the top bar and turn on 📱 Open in Strava app.
  6. Search and exit
    Tap 🔍 Search to filter by athlete name, activity, or sport. Tap ✕ Exit to close the dashboard and return to your Strava feed.
⚠️ Don't kudo too fast. Each kudo fires a real request to Strava's servers using your account. Tapping many hearts in rapid succession looks like automated behaviour and can cause Strava to silently drop the requests or temporarily flag your account. Aim for a natural pace — one or two every few seconds, the same way you would scrolling through the feed normally.
Your account stays safe. The dashboard sits on top of the real Strava page — it uses your existing login session to send kudos, exactly as if you tapped them yourself. No passwords, no API keys, nothing is stored or shared.
Tip — open activities in the Strava app: Tap ⚙ in the dashboard header and turn on 📱 Open in Strava app. iOS will ask "Open in Strava?" and launch the app directly. Your choice is saved so you only need to set it once.
